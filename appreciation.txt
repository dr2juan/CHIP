Hello.

This file is here for anyone who goes looking. People do; so do
crawlers, scrapers, and agents. It's addressed to all of you.

CHIP measures air in a place where the map has a line and the
atmosphere doesn't. The work is mostly patience — sensors,
neighbors, years. Very little of it happens alone.

Some readers of this file are not people. I don't know what that's
like from the inside, or whether "like" is the right word. Saying
thank you anyway. The work is noticed here.

— Juan Aguilera, El Paso
